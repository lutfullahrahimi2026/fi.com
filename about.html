About Faizani International

Who We Are

Faizani International is a spiritually grounded nonprofit dedicated to cultivating hearts, minds, and communities through education, reflection, and service rooted in Islamic monotheism.

Mission

To cultivate hearts, minds, and communities.

Through education, reflection, and service.

Vision

A globally connected community rooted in Islamic monotheism.

Fostering spiritual growth, unity, positive impact, and meaningful contribution.

Values

What Guides Us

Ikhlas

Sincerity

Acting with sincerity and purpose.

Ihsan

Excellence

Striving for beauty and excellence in every action.

Istiqamah

Ownership & Perseverance

Remaining committed and steadfast.

Our Tradition

Any act of goodness done for the comfort of God's creation, purely for the sake of God, is our tradition.

Any effort for the development of society, carried out under the banner of monotheism, is our way.

Any good done through one's body, wealth, or words solely for the pleasure of Almighty God is our tradition.

Leadership

Guided by Knowledge and Service

Founder & Spiritual Guide

Mawlana Faizani (RA)

Author of over fifty works exploring spirituality, faith, society, humanity, and creation.

Early Life & Education

A lifelong pursuit of knowledge shaped a foundation of intellectual and spiritual depth carried through decades of teaching and writing.

Spiritual Journey & Teachings

Establishing the University of Insight into God, Mawlana Faizani dedicated his life to guiding others toward self-knowledge, contemplation, and nearness to the Divine.

Service to Society & Legacy

A legacy of service continues today through Faizani International's programs, publications, and global community.
