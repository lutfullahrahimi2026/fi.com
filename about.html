About Faizani International

Who We Are

Faizani International is a spiritually grounded nonprofit rooted in Islam and the teachings of the twentieth-century Muslim scholar Mawlana Faizani (RA). We cultivate hearts and minds, and communities through remembrance, contemplation, and service.

Mission

To cultivate hearts, minds, and communities through spiritual education and practices rooted in Islam.

Inspiring purposeful living and meaningful contribution to society.

Vision

To create a globally connected community rooted in Islamic monotheism.

Fostering spiritual growth, unity, and positive impact across cultures working for the welfare of humanity.

Values

Our Tradition

Any act of goodness done for the comfort of God's creation, purely for the sake of God, is our tradition.

Any effort for the development of society, carried out under the banner of monotheism, is our way.

At any place, for anyone, in any society, any good done through body, wealth, or words, solely for the pleasure of Almighty God and without any ulterior motive, is our tradition.

Founder & Spiritual Guide

Mawlana Mohammad Attaullah Faizani (RA)

Author of over fifty works exploring spirituality, faith, society, humanity, and creation.

Early Life & Education

Mawlana Mohammad Attaullah Faizani (1923–1979) was a renowned Islamic scholar, spiritual teacher, and reformer from Herat, Afghanistan. Born into a family of religious scholars, he pursued both traditional and modern education before dedicating his life to the pursuit of spiritual knowledge and service.

Spiritual Journey & Teachings

After years of study and travel across the Islamic world, Mawlana Faizani (RA) underwent a period of deep spiritual retreat, engaging in rigorous practices of remembrance (dhikr) and contemplation (fikr). His teachings emphasize the integration of faith (iman) with intellect (aql), encouraging individuals to understand belief through reflection on both revelation and the natural world.

He attracted a diverse following of students, professionals, and seekers, uniting people across sectarian lines and inspiring a movement centered on spiritual growth, self-discipline, and service to society. His work laid the foundation for what became known as the University of Insight into God, a unique approach that combines spiritual practice with intellectual inquiry.

Service to Society & Legacy

Despite facing repeated imprisonment due to his outspoken criticism of injustice and corruption, Mawlana Faizani (RA) remained committed to peaceful reform and the transformation of society through personal and collective spiritual development. During his lifetime, he authored over fifty works on spirituality, faith, and society, many of which continue to guide and inspire his followers today.

His legacy lives on through his teachings, his writings, and the global community that continues to carry forward his vision of a spiritually grounded and purposeful life.

President

Ustadh Mazhabi Faizani

Ustadh Mazhabi, born in Kabul, Afghanistan in 1964, is a distinguished Islamic educator and spiritual leader, and the eldest son of Mawlana Faizani (RA). From a young age, he continued his education with determination despite political instability and personal hardships, completing degrees with honors in Islamic Studies and Economics.

Committed to spiritual and intellectual development, Ustadh Mazhabi has dedicated his life to guiding individuals and communities through teachings rooted in remembrance (dhikr), contemplation (fikr), and service (khidmah). He has worked extensively to promote a balanced understanding of faith, one that integrates spiritual practice with intellectual reflection and engagement with the modern world.

Throughout his life, he has led initiatives focused on education, community development, and humanitarian service, including the establishment of libraries, publications, and programs supporting refugees and underserved communities. He has also engaged with scholars, leaders, and international organizations to advocate for peace, unity, and social responsibility.

As a teacher, speaker, and reformer, Ustadh Mazhabi emphasizes personal transformation as the foundation for societal change. His message centers on unity, compassion, and the universal relevance of spiritual principles in addressing modern challenges.

Today, he continues to lead and expand the work of the University of Insight into God, carrying forward the legacy of Mawlana Faizani (RA) and inspiring a global community committed to faith and service.
